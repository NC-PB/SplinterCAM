# AQ-010: Review of the ncx SPEC draft (plan 0007, step 6)

- Asked: 2026-10-27, plan 0007 step 6 (SPEC draft, lane B2), by the coding agent (headless run)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: a reviewed SPEC is a human gate; what reaches the machine; a departure from Peter's `ROUND_LIMIT` answer of 2026-10-02; an exception to docs/dev/03)
- Question: `src/splintercam/ncx/SPEC.md` (25 requirements, REQ-NCX-001 to 025, all Draft) writes the slice's NCX from the `cl` record and runs `ncx format --check`, `check --strict` and `compile`. One spec review and one CNC review are folded in. Please confirm or change each choice:
  1. **No `ROUND_LIMIT` in the program while the pin is f74559c** (REQ-NCX-009, DEC-NCX-003). Your answer to NCXchange's D363 says the CAM writes `ROUND_LIMIT=0.1·tol` per operation, but f74559c does not have D363 yet (research 27, Checked condition 3), and an unknown word most likely fails `check` (RR-006 asks). The writer computes the allowance and holds it; the word comes with the pin that has D363. On the machine: nothing changes, `ROUND_LIMIT` has no control form.
  2. **`COOLANT=OFF` and `SPINDLE=OFF` before `PROGRAM=END`** (REQ-NCX-001, DEC-NCX-008). Research 26's mapping has no spindle or coolant off, and nothing in the research says that the iTNC 530's program end stops them. Without the words the program could end with the spindle turning and coolant on, 5 mm above the part. `SPINDLE=OFF` is not confirmed as an NCX value (RR-006); the integration test with the real `ncx` checks it.
  3. **`CMP020` counted as one INFO per operation; every other `compile` warning fails the run** (REQ-NCX-022, DEC-NCX-006). Until D363, nothing checks the machine's rounding against the allowance. On a three-decimal file the worst point moves √3·0.0005 ≈ 0.00087 mm over three axes, inside the allowance 0.001 mm at tol 0.01; below a tol of about 0.0087 mm it can exceed the allowance unseen (D-149 gives about 0.0071 mm, the figure for two axes, √2·0.0005). Options: (a) accept that for the slice (tol 0.01 in the golden case and your dry run); (b) refuse tol below 0.0087 mm in the slice (a figure for 0.001 mm machines only); (c) read the machine file's decimals and check in the CAM (machine data in `ncx`, more code).
  4. **One tool and one spindle speed per program** (REQ-NCX-003, DEC-NCX-004). What a second `TOOL` block at the clearance height, or a speed change without `TOOL`, compiles to has not been checked against NCXchange; the slice has one tool. A job with two tools is refused with `NCX_TOOL_CHANGE_UNSUPPORTED`.
  5. **`OFFSET:LEN=<tool number>` on the Siemens file** (research 27, trap 11): `OFFSET:LEN=5` is `H5` on Fanuc but cutting edge D5 of the active tool on Siemens. For T1 that is D1, as on your iTNC 530 slice; for T5 on an 840D sl it would select D5 of T5, likely an edge nobody measured. Your decision of 2026-10-11 (plan 0007, Decisions) stands; the point words of D-155 (R1-11) fix it. Recommended: no change in the slice; a line for the Siemens dry runs of later plans.
  6. **Two exceptions to docs/dev/03, rule 5** (DEC-NCX-001, 007): waiting for `ncx` polls the cancel flag on a timer (the clock, below layer 6), and `ncx compile` writes the controller files, into a staging folder `job` made and empties. Both seem unavoidable for "runs the pinned ncx binary" in `architecture/modules.yaml`.
  7. **Program names**: ASCII letters, digits and underscores, not a Windows device name (`CON`, `NUL`, `COM1`, …), number ≥ 1 (REQ-NCX-014).
  For your dry run (step 8), the CNC review lists what to look for in `RECT_FILLETS.h`: `TOOL CALL 1 Z S8000` before any `L` block; the first motion `L Z+5 … FMAX` without X and Y; M3 and M8 before the first feed move; cycle 247 with Q339 = 1 before the first move; every arc `DR-` about the fillet centres; M5 and M9 (from item 2), then `END PGM`. NCX has no stock word, so there is no `BLK FORM` (research 27): the control's test-run graphics may need a blank set by hand. The clearance height is 5 mm over the stock; clamps are not modelled, so a clamp higher than that is in the way of the rapid of move 1.
