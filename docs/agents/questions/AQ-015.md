# AQ-015: Review of the toolpath SPEC draft, the slice's links (plan 0007, step 5)

- Asked: 2026-10-27, plan 0007 step 5, by the coding agent (headless run, lane B1)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: a reviewed SPEC is a human gate; the links decide where the tool moves at rapid and how it goes down beside the stock)
- Question: `src/splintercam/toolpath/SPEC.md` (14 requirements, REQ-TP-001 to 014, all Draft) builds the moves around a strategy's passes, as research 10 and your answer to plan 0007, question 4 say: after the tool change, a rapid up or down along Z to the clearance height h_c = stock top + 5 mm; a rapid in the plane at h_c over the approach point P; per pass, a rapid down to h_r = stock top + 3 mm at P, a feed down at the plunge feed to the pass's depth at P, the pass, and a rapid up to h_c at P. No rapid in the plane runs below h_c. A broken plan from a strategy is refused by raising (DEC-TP-002). Five points change what happens on the machine; please confirm or change each:
  1. **Clamps and vice jaws are not known to the CAM** (research 10, trap 1; the spec and cnc reviews). The rapid from the tool-change position to P runs at stock top + 5 mm, and the descent at P passes the stock's side about R + 0.2·R from its nominal face, down to the full depth. A clamp higher than stock top + 5 mm anywhere under that rapid is hit at rapid traverse. A vice jaw beside the face where P lies is hit at the plunge feed. With the profile's start on the longest side (AQ-016, item 4), P lies by a long side, the side a vice grips. Options: (a) the setup sheet (`job` or `ncx`, steps 6 and 7) states "nothing higher than stock top + 5 mm, no clamp within R + 0.2·R of the stock's sides to the full depth; the stock held from below or raised above the jaws by more than the depth", and your dry run (step 8) checks it; (b) as (a), and also put P by a short side (AQ-016, item 4); (c) a keep-out face per stock side entered in the job, refused at P: new requirements and a model field, a later plan. Recommended: (a) for the slice, with (b) decided in AQ-016.
  2. **The heights are fixed at stock top + 5 and + 3 mm (D-062), with no job parameter** (REQ-TP-001, DEC-TP-001). A shop that needs more clearance over a tall clamp cannot raise it in the slice. Options: (a) fixed, as D-062; (b) a job parameter for h_c (a `model` field and a change to `cl`'s header source). Recommended: (a); (b) with the fixtures of a later plan.
  3. **The order after the tool change: Z first, then across at h_c** (REQ-TP-003, as `cl`'s Reviewed REQ-CL-006 and 012 and research 10, Method 1). The cnc review notes the usual safer order on many machines: across at the tool-change height, then down along Z at P. With Z first, the tool comes down at the unknown tool-change position to stock top + 5 mm and then crosses at that height; with "across first" it crosses at the tool-change height, which the CAM does not know (it is the machine's). Options: (a) keep research 10's order (the record has no tool-change height to cross at); (b) change research 10, `cl` and toolpath so the first move is in the plane: needs the machine's tool-change height from NCXchange's machine file (D-053). Recommended: (a) for the slice, and check it on the iTNC 530 in the dry run; (b) as a research request if the dry run shows the iTNC moves Z down at the tool change.
  4. **The descent at P is a feed move all the way from h_r to the pass's depth** (REQ-TP-005; research 10, Method 2), beside the stock in air. It takes (3 mm + depth) / plunge feed per pass: about 2.6 s at 13 mm and 300 mm/min, three passes in research 22 test 1. If the real stock is oversize or placed off its nominal box by more than the air gap (1 mm at R = 5), the tool plunges axially into solid at that feed: a tool that is not centre cutting can break (cnc review). Options: (a) as drafted, with the air gap of D-079, and the setup sheet says "stock within 1 mm of its box"; (b) a larger air gap default for the slice (your number; research 22's default is D-079's 0.2·R); (c) refuse tools that are not centre cutting (`model` has no such field yet). Recommended: (a); the user can raise `air_gap_ratio` per operation.
  5. **A descent within R of the stock box is refused** (REQ-TP-012, DEC-TP-004): at P the tool may not touch the stock's side or corner on the way down; touching counts as a collision. The box is grown by R with round corners, so P diagonally off a corner is accepted where the tool really clears it.
- Recommendation: approve the SPEC as drafted, with 1 (a), 2 (a), 3 (a), 4 (a) and 5 as drafted. Safe default meanwhile: nothing is implemented; DEC-TP-001 to 004 stand as provisional.
- Status: Answered 2026-10-27
- Answer: approved as drafted, 1 (a), 2 (a), 3 (a), 4 (a) and 5 as drafted (Peter): Z first after the tool change, checked in the dry run; the setup sheet states the clamp and jaw limits; heights fixed per D-062. Recorded in docs/plans/active/0007-first-nc-program.md, Decisions.
