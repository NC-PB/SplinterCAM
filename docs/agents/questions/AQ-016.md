# AQ-016: Review of the profile SPEC draft (plan 0007, step 5)

- Asked: 2026-10-27, plan 0007 step 5, by the coding agent (headless run, lane B1)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: a reviewed SPEC is a human gate; the choices below decide what the machine cuts and which jobs are refused)
- Question: `src/splintercam/strategies/profile/SPEC.md` (22 requirements, REQ-PRF-001 to 022, all Draft) plans the outside profile as research 22 says: the tool centre path from offset2d's grow of the part by R + allowance, clockwise for climb with the spindle CW, equal depth passes, a start point S on the path and an approach point P beside the stock, approach P → S at the plunge feed, once round, retract S → P at the cutting feed, the links through toolpath (AQ-015). Eight points change the NC program or which jobs are refused; please confirm or change each:
  1. **The fillets are cut as short straight G1 moves, no G2/G3** (REQ-PRF-011, DEC-PRF-001). offset2d returns a polygon, and D-023's arc fitting is out of plan 0007. On research 22 test 1 each R 10 fillet becomes a few G1 blocks within the chord tolerance (a = 0.05·tol); the path is never closer than R + allowance to the true contour, so no gouge; the program is longer and the control may slow a little at the fillets. Options: (a) lines in the slice, arcs with the arc fit (topic 11) in a later plan; (b) one G2/G3 per fillet rebuilt from the source arc (an arc fit of our own without research, wrong where offset2d joins corners). Recommended: (a).
  2. **The depth for the flute-length check is measured from the stock top, not from z_top** (REQ-PRF-007, DEC-PRF-004). The CAM does not know whether the material above z_top was removed, so it assumes the tool may be in stock from the stock top down to z_bottom. Where z_top is the stock top (the slice's examples) both readings agree. Recommended: as drafted.
  3. **A first pass deeper than the step-down below the stock top is refused** (REQ-PRF-005, `PROFILE_FIRST_PASS_DEEP`; spec and cnc review). Example: stock top +0.5 (facing allowance), z_top 0, step-down 1: the first pass at −1 cuts 1.5 mm, 50 % over the set step. Options: (a) refuse, the user sets z_top to the stock top (drafted, conservative); (b) a warning naming the first pass's depth, the program is written; (c) count the passes from the stock top whenever z_top lies below it (the levels change from research 22's). Recommended: (a) for the slice. Levels above the stock top are accepted (passes in air cost time only), as long as the first level lies below stock top + 3 mm.
  4. **The start S lies in the middle of the longest straight side** (REQ-PRF-013; research 22, Method 4). The approach, the descent at P and the witness mark at S are on that side. In a vice the long sides are the gripped sides, so the descent runs past a jaw (AQ-015, item 1; cnc and spec review). Options: (a) as research 22 (longest straight side); (b) the middle of the longest straight side among the shorter sides of the stock box (by a short side, away from vice jaws); (c) a start the user picks (a later plan). Recommended: (a) for the slice with the setup sheet of AQ-015 (a), since the profile also cuts 5 mm of air outside every stock face on research 22 test 1 (the path at part + 5, the stock at part + 5, the tool reaching part + 10): side clamping is not possible for this program anyway, the stock must be held from below or raised above the jaws.
  5. **A z_bottom below the stock bottom is refused** (REQ-PRF-006, `PROFILE_BELOW_STOCK`). Below the stock are the parallels, the vice or the table; the slice models no fixtures (research 10, trap 1). A through cut (z_bottom 0.5 mm below the stock bottom onto a spoil board) is therefore not possible in the slice. z_bottom equal to the stock bottom is accepted: the tip then runs at the face the stock sits on, and a tool-length error marks it (cnc review). Options: (a) refuse below, accept equal (drafted); (b) also warn within a margin above the stock bottom (your margin); (c) accept below with a warning naming how far. Recommended: (a); a spoil board with a later fixture model.
  6. **A step-down up to the flute length is accepted** (REQ-PRF-008; research 22, Parameters: at most the flute length). On a D 10 with 30 mm of flute, 30 mm per pass is valid, and the straight approach P → S then slots the full tool width at that depth (cnc review). There is no cutting-data check until the cutting-data solver. Options: (a) as research 22; (b) a warning above a limit from a tool maker's data (needs a research request). Recommended: (a); the dry run uses the slice's 4 mm.
  7. **Approach and retract are straight lines along the normal at S** (REQ-PRF-014, 017; research 22, Method 6): the approach at the plunge feed (it may enter the stock allowance with the full tool width), the retract at the cutting feed through the stretch the approach cut. With no lead arc, the part shows a witness mark at S on every pass. Where S already lies clear of the stock by R + 0.2·R (a large allowance or a stock flush with the part), P is S and the tool goes down beside the stock straight onto the path, with no approach line. Recommended: as drafted; leads with topic 10's later parts.
  8. **Where the normal at S would cross the part (a U whose inner side is the longest), S moves to the path's smallest point** (REQ-PRF-015, DEC-PRF-003; spec review). At that point the way out is always free, so the approach never cuts across the part; the start is then a corner or a curved stretch rather than the middle of a side. Recommended: as drafted.
- Recommendation: approve the SPEC as drafted, with 1 (a), 2 as drafted, 3 (a), 4 (a), 5 (a), 6 (a), 7 and 8 as drafted. Safe default meanwhile: nothing is implemented; DEC-PRF-001 to 006 stand as provisional. RR-007 asks Project Spike to word research 22's tests for the polygonal path.
