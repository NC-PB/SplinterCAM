# AQ-021: Should toolpath also refuse a pass below the stock bottom (plan 0007, step 5)

- Asked: 2026-10-27, plan 0007 step 5 (`toolpath` implementation, cnc-reviewer finding 2), by the coding agent (headless run, lane E)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: machine safety, what lies below the stock, and a new rule in the Reviewed `src/splintercam/toolpath/SPEC.md`)
- Question: `link_operation` checks only that each level z_i is finite and below h_r (REQ-TP-010). A level of z = −500 under a 15 mm stock passes, and the descent at P feeds the tool beside the stock down into the vice or the table. The profile already refuses a z_bottom below the stock bottom with `PROFILE_BELOW_STOCK` (REQ-PRF-006; your answer to AQ-016, item 5 (a): below the stock is the table or the vice), so this only matters if a strategy has a bug or a later strategy forgets the check. Options:
  - (a) Add to REQ-TP-010: IF a pass's z_i lies below the stock bottom (`stock.min_mm[2]`), THEN `link_operation` SHALL raise `ValueError` naming the pass. A backstop for every strategy, the same limit as REQ-PRF-006 (z_i = stock bottom allowed). About 2 NLOC; unit tests at the bottom and one ulp below it. A spoil board or a through cut later loosens both checks together with the fixture model (D-078).
  - (b) Leave it to the strategies (REQ-PRF-006 for the profile); toolpath trusts its levels.
- Recommendation: (a): one comparison, and toolpath is the one place every strategy's descent passes. Safe default meanwhile: the Reviewed SPEC as it stands (no such check); the profile, the only producer in plan 0007, refuses it before a plan exists.
- Also for the setup sheet and the dry run (step 7 and 8, no question, from the same review): P lies R + 0.2·R beside the stock on the side of the start point S, and the tool goes below the stock top there; in a vice this is often inside the jaw's footprint, more likely to be hit than a clamp above h_c. The setup sheet (AQ-015, item 1 (a)) should state "P is X mm beside the stock on side S; nothing may stand there between h_r and z_bottom". The dry run should also watch the first move after the tool change, a rapid along Z to h_c at the tool-change position (research 10, trap 2).
