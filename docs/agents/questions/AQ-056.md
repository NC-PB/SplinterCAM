# AQ-056: What the slice's setup sheet holds, and the keep-out numbers (plan 0009)

- Asked: 2026-11-08, plan 0009 step 1 (SPEC draft, lane S), by the coding agent (headless run)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: what the operator is told; machine safety)
- Question: `src/splintercam/setupsheet/SPEC.md` (REQ-SHT-001 to 019, Draft) puts AQ-035's notes 1 to 8 on the sheet as numbers computed from the job and the toolpath record (table in `docs/plans/active/0009-setup-sheet.md`). Five points change what the operator reads; please confirm or change each:
  1. **The keep-out region: the box the tool reaches, and a margin round it.** Below the clearance height the sheet computes from the toolpath the box of every tool-centre position grown by R, from the lowest Z to the clearance height (the reach; at its edge the tool's side just touches; DEC-SHT-002). The two earlier statements were the profile's formulas and differ from it on every side. For the golden job (part X 0 to 100, Y 0 to 60; stock X −5 to 105, Y −5 to 65; P at Y −11):

     | | −X | +X | −Y (P's side) | +Y |
     | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
     | Reach (tool edge) | −10 | 110 | −16 | 70 |
     | AQ-035 item 2: 15 mm round the part | −15 | 115 | −15 | 75 |
     | AQ-015 item 1 (a): R + 0.2·R off the stock | −11 | 111 | −11 | 71 |
     | Reach + 5 mm | −15 | 115 | −21 | 75 |

     The 15 mm band is the tool's edge (10 mm off the part) plus 5 mm, but it misses the tool at P by 1 mm; AQ-015's figure is 1 mm wider than the reach on three sides and misses P by 5 mm. Options: (a) the exact reach only, the operator keeps their own distance; (b) the reach grown by a margin, your number, printed as the only box; (c) both: the reach ("the tool's edge comes this far") and a keep-clear box, the reach grown by your margin ("no clamp, jaw or vice part inside"). Recommended: (c) with 5 mm, the margin your AQ-035 band already had, now also round P. The sheet also says, where the reach covers the stock in XY (as here), "hold the stock from below, or grip it only below Z −10" (AQ-015 item 1 (a), AQ-016 item 4).
  2. **The work offset is printed as its index**, "work offset 1 (NCX ORIGIN=1)", not as the control's code (cycle 247 Q339 = 1 on the iTNC 530, G54 on Fanuc). The controller's name is NCXchange's knowledge (D-148); research 27's Datum row has it, but copying that table into the CAM is the second copy D-148 forbids. RR-023 asks whether NCXchange can provide it. Options: (a) index only until NCXchange provides the name; (b) the controller's name now, from research 27's table, for the three shipped machines. Recommended: (a).
  3. **Material, programmer and date are empty fields to fill in by hand.** The job file has none of them and a run date would make two runs of the same job differ (D-055). Options: (a) empty fields; (b) leave them out; (c) the date of the run (two runs differ; tests must ignore it). Recommended: (a).
  4. **AQ-035 item 5 (every pass is a full slot where the stock margin exceeds s + 2R) is left off.** It tells the operator about the tool's load, which they cannot change at the setup, and computing it for any strategy needs the stock model's engagement (later plan). Options: (a) left off until the stock model; (b) a profile-only line from the stock margin and the pass offsets. Recommended: (a).
  5. **"Not yet run on a machine: dry run first, with this sheet in hand."** stands at the top of every sheet. The CAM cannot know whether a program has been proven. Options: (a) on every sheet in this plan; (b) only until you decide AQ-059 item 1. Recommended: (a); proven programs need a record of their own (a later plan).
  Also left out of the feature map's row in the slice (no question unless you want one now): the part and clamping pictures, holders and stick-out, cutting times and totals, the previous setup's stock, free notes, PDF, templates (Q-137 item 5), translations.
- Recommendation: 1 (c) with 5 mm, 2 (a), 3 (a), 4 (a), 5 (a). Safe default taken: these, in the SPEC draft (`src/splintercam/setupsheet/SPEC.md`, REQ-SHT-012 holds the 5 mm as provisional); nothing is coded before the answer.
