# AQ-059: What the setup sheet changes in AQ-035's "until a setup sheet exists" rules (plan 0009)

- Asked: 2026-11-08, plan 0009 step 1 (SPEC draft, lane S), by the coding agent (headless run)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: who runs the programs; machine safety; a refusal Peter chose)
- Question: Your answer to AQ-035 set two rules that end when a setup sheet exists:
  1. **"No program of the slice goes to an operator other than Peter until a setup sheet exists."** Options: (a) the rule ends when plan 0009's step 4 is on `main` (every program then has its sheet); (b) it ends after your dry run with the golden job's sheet in hand (plan 0009, step 6) shows the sheet tells an operator what they need; (c) it stays until a later plan records proven programs.
  2. **`ncx` refuses a measurement point with an axial offset a ≠ 0 (REQ-NCX-013, `NCX_POINT_OFFSET_UNSUPPORTED`; DEC-NCX-018) "until the setup sheet or D-155's point words exist".** With the sheet, "T1 measured 3 mm above the tip" is printed (REQ-SHT-008), but the NC program still does not say it, and a tool measured at the tip instead cuts every depth 3 mm deep with no message. Options: (a) keep the refusal in this plan; lifting it is its own `ncx` change once you decide, ideally with the point words (R1-11); (b) lift it with step 4: the sheet is the only place that says where to measure. The slice's tools are all measured at the tip, so (a) costs nothing today.
- Recommendation: 1 (b): the sheet is new and the dry run is the only check of what it says; 2 (a): a wrong a moves every Z of the program, and the sheet alone is easy to leave on the desk. Safe default taken: both rules stand unchanged; `docs/plans/active/0009-setup-sheet.md` step 6 stops for your decision on item 1; nothing in `src/splintercam/ncx/` changes in plan 0009.
- Status: Answered 2026-10-27
- Answer: item 1 (b) as recommended (Peter): programs go to other operators only after his dry run with a sheet. Item 2 is superseded by AQ-051 (Peter, 2026-11-08): k ≠ 1 and a ≠ 0 are allowed, the program carries a comment naming the point and a before the tool change, and the sheet repeats it.
