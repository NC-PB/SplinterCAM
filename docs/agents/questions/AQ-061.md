# AQ-061: A CIRCLE on the pocket's layer is a wall (an island or a round pocket), not a drilled hole (plan 0008, step 2)

- Asked: 2026-11-08, plan 0008 step 2 (SPEC draft, lane IO), by the coding agent (headless run)
- Box: 1 (Peter's judgement: an accepted decision, D-027, and what the user sees)
- Question: D-027 says DXF circles are holes, grouped by diameter, with hole type, depth and bottom set per group in a dialog (a drilling feature, later). The pocket's golden case (research 04, test 1) draws its round island as a circle of radius 6. io's chaining already treats a full CIRCLE as a closed chain of its own (REQ-IO-018), so on the pocket's layer it becomes a loop, and the pocket mills round it.
  - (a) **On the layer the pocket reads, every circle is a wall like any other loop** (an island inside the outer loop, or the outer wall of a round pocket). D-027's grouping applies to circles the user selects for drilling, a later plan, which reads its own layer or selection. On the machine: a circle meant as a drilled hole but drawn on the pocket's layer leaves a round boss of material standing in the pocket, visible in the preview and in "2 loops" of the import report; nothing is cut into it.
  - (b) **Circles on the pocket's layer are refused** with a new `DXF_CIRCLE_IN_REGION` (error) until D-027's dialog exists: a round island must then be drawn as two arcs or a polyline with bulges, which CAD users rarely do; the golden case would need redrawing.
  - (c) **Circles are skipped with a warning** and the rest read: the pocket is then cut through the island, so material the drawing keeps is milled away. Not recommended.
- Recommendation: (a). It costs nothing in io and matches the golden case; the user separates drill holes from pocket walls by layer, as the profile already requires (`src/splintercam/io/SPEC.md`, Example parts: hole circles on the outline's layer stop the profile). Safe default taken: (a) in `src/splintercam/io/SPEC.md` (Scope, Later parts), no new requirement.
