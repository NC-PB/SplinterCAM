# AQ-066: Should the setup sheet also list descents at feed from h_c as approach points? (plan 0009)

- Asked: 2026-11-08, plan 0009 step 2 (`src/splintercam/setupsheet/`, cnc review M1), by the coding agent (headless run)
- Box: 1 (what the setter is told; which descents the sheet refuses near the stock: machine safety)
- Question: REQ-SHT-010 (Reviewed) makes an approach point only where a RAPID goes down and ends below the clearance height h_c. `cl` also accepts an operation that rapids across at h_c and then goes straight down at feed (a LINE along Z alone from h_c, no rapid to h_r). On the machine the tool then comes down beside or into the stock at feed, but the sheet lists no approach point there: no "the tool comes down here, nothing may stand here" line, no side and gap, and no refusal if the place is within R of the stock box (REQ-SHT-011). Options: (a) also make an approach point at every LINE along Z alone that goes down from h_c or above and ends below h_c, with the same side, gap and refusal; (b) keep REQ-SHT-010 as it is until a strategy writes such a descent, and revisit then.
- Recommendation: (a) before the first strategy that descends at feed from h_c; it is about 5 lines in `src/splintercam/setupsheet/_approach.py` and one SPEC sentence. Safe default taken: (b), the Reviewed wording, which is complete for every record `toolpath` writes today; noted in `src/splintercam/setupsheet/AGENTS.md`.
