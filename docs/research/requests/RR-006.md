# RR-006: The canonical NCX layout, and `ROUND_LIMIT` and `SPINDLE=OFF` at the pin

- Date: 2026-10-27; raised by: plan 0007, step 6 (`ncx` SPEC draft, lane B2)
- Question: (1) What exactly does `ncx format --check` require at NCXchange f74559c: the order of words within a block (the canonical ranks of `CanonicalRanks.cs`, for the words of `src/splintercam/ncx/SPEC.md`, REQ-NCX-001 to 006), the spacing, the comment column, blank lines, the line end and a final line end? (2) Does the pinned `ncx` know the word `ROUND_LIMIT` (NCXchange D363, answered 2026-10-02), and what does `check --strict` say about it if not? (3) Are `SPINDLE=OFF` and `COOLANT=OFF` valid NCX, and what do the three shipped mill files write for `PROGRAM=END` (does the program end switch the spindle and coolant off on the iTNC 530: `M30`, `M2`, `END PGM`)?
- Why it matters: REQ-NCX-011 (the writer emits canonical text, so `format --check` passes without piping through `ncx format`); REQ-NCX-009 and DEC-NCX-003 (`ROUND_LIMIT` held back at the pin); DEC-NCX-008 and AQ-010 item 2 (the spindle stop and coolant off before the program end).
- What is known: `docs/research/26-toolpath-record.md`, Number printer ("`format --check` checks word order, spacing and the comment column") and Mapping onto NCX; `docs/research/27-machines-and-controllers.md`, Checked against NCXchange (the sample program passed `format --check`; its text is not in the research) and condition 3 (D363 not implemented at f74559c). Nothing on the ranks, on `ROUND_LIMIT` at the pin, or on `SPINDLE=OFF`.
- Provisional choice: the writer follows research 26's mapping in the order given there, one space between words, and the end-to-end `format --check` is the oracle (REQ-NCX-011); `ROUND_LIMIT` not written (DEC-NCX-003); `COOLANT=OFF` and `SPINDLE=OFF` written before `PROGRAM=END` (DEC-NCX-008), checked by the real-`ncx` integration test of `src/splintercam/ncx/SPEC.md`. The sample program of research 27's check, if it is kept in Project Spike, would answer (1) at once.
- Status: Answered on 2026-10-27 from NCXchange at the pin f74559c (`git show f74559c:...` in NC-PB/NCXchange_): (1) the order of words is the rank of `docs/spec/generated/word-catalog.md` (for example `PROGRAM` 40, `NAME` 60, `NUMBER` 70, `SPINDLE` 480, `COOLANT` 570, `TOLERANCE` 790, `STOP` 1080); the block layout is `docs/spec/ncx-language.md` section 5, rule 7: the words separated by one space, a comment after the words with its semicolon in column 57, or three spaces after the words when they reach column 54 or later; blank lines and comment-only lines are kept as read (D92). Line end and final line end are not stated in the language document; the end-to-end `ncx format --check` stays the oracle (REQ-NCX-011). (2) `ROUND_LIMIT` is not in the word catalog at f74559c, so `check` refuses it as an unknown word: DEC-NCX-003 (not written) is right. (3) `SPINDLE=OFF` (M5) and `COOLANT=OFF` (M9) are valid values at the pin (catalog ranks 480 and 570). `machines/heidenhain-itnc530.toml` writes `M30` before `END PGM` for `PROGRAM=END`; whether M30 also stops the spindle and coolant is machine data not stated there, so writing both words before `PROGRAM=END` (DEC-NCX-008) stays the safe choice and can now be Accepted in full.
