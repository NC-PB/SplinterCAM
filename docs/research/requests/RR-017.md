# RR-017: What `ncx check` and `ncx compile` print on stdout at the pin

- Date: 2026-10-30; raised by: the CNC review of `src/splintercam/ncx/` (lane F3, branch `ncx/fail-closed-and-point-a`)
- Question: At NCXchange f74559c, what do `ncx check <file> --machine <toml> --strict` and `ncx compile <file> --machine <toml> --output <dir>` write to stdout, on success and on failure? Can a WARNING or an ERROR ever go to stdout instead of stderr (for example a message of the machine-file loader, an unhandled exception, or a summary line), and is stdout empty when the step passes?
- Why it matters: `run_ncx` reads only stderr for diagnostics and fails closed on any WARNING or unread line there (REQ-NCX-020, 022, 027; DEC-NCX-014 (e), DEC-NCX-017). stdout goes only to the debug log (DEC-NCX-007), apart from `format --check`'s difference, which is in `NCX_STEP_FAILED`. A warning that went to stdout with exit 0 would pass unseen. If stdout is always empty on a pass, `run_ncx` could also fail closed on any non-empty stdout of `check` and `compile`. If it holds a normal summary, that would fail every run.
- What is known: `docs/research/27-machines-and-controllers.md`, Exit codes: "Diagnostics go to stderr, one per line". Nothing on stdout. The stderr form itself is not yet checked against the pin (AQ-025; the real-`ncx` test `tests/ncx/integration/test_real_ncx.py` is skipped in CI until it builds `ncx`).
- Provisional choice: as research 27 says, the diagnostics are read from stderr only, and stdout is logged at debug level. Once the real-`ncx` test runs in CI, it can assert what stdout holds on the golden program.
- Status: Open
