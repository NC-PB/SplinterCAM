# Spec delta for plan 0001, 2026-10-01

> **Updated after Q-133 (D-132, CP-005).** The prototype had moved on since the snapshot of 2026-09-27 (grid of one length epsilon, join tolerance of half the offset share, bias e + 3 and band e + 6 length epsilons, REQ-OFF-013 to 015 released). D-132 keeps D-058's clearance, side and 10⁴ grid, and takes over the prototype's rounding margin and requirements. The rows below follow D-132. Timing: apply them when the kernel is next touched, after the decision gate of plan 0001 (D-133). Background: [reviews/2026-10-01-prototype-insights.md](../reviews/2026-10-01-prototype-insights.md).

Plan 0001 runs in test_repo on the specs snapshot of 2026-09-27 (D-009). Since then, the decisions of 2026-09-28 changed what the foundation and geometry2d specs require. This note lists every change that touches the prototype, so it does not harden a convention we have already replaced. The updated specs: [foundation](foundation-SPEC.md), [geometry2d](geometry2d-SPEC.md).

How to read the last column: **now** means cheap to adopt in the current step and costly later; **room** means the prototype need not build it, but the array layout or the API must leave room for it; **gate** means it matters for the decision gate of plan 0001.
