| D-146 | 2026-10-01 | Tolerance budget at small tolerances (Q-132): the shares of D-056 keep their meaning, with two floors. Geometry gets 0.1·tol plus what the grid costs: the 6 grid units of D-132's rounding margin, plus max(0, 2 grid units − 0.05·tol) for the floor of the arc tolerance a; the offset uses a + 6 grid units of it, the remaining 0.05·tol covers flattening and snapping. The rounding reserve is max(0.1·tol, (√k/2)·s), with s the smallest output step the machine file declares for the linear axes and k the number of rounded axes in a move (2 planar, 3 in 3D). The control keeps 0.5·tol; the arc-fit band gets the rest. When the rest is negative, the tolerance is below the smallest supported value and the operation is refused with a diagnostic that names that value: about 0.0034 mm at an output step of 0.001 mm (0.0037 mm in 3D), 0.0023 mm at 0.0001 mm, 0.0060 mm at 0.0001 inch. The defaults of D-029 stay; at 0.01 mm the band is 0.0024 mm with metric and 0.0016 mm with inch output. Tests run at 0.01 mm and at the smallest supported tolerance for each output step of the reference machines, instead of 0.001 mm; kernels also get unit tests with exact values. | D-148 | 2026-10-01 | Controller independence (Peter): the CAM writes the same NCX for every controller, the same words, moves and values whether the machine runs Heidenhain, Siemens, Fanuc or another control. All controller knowledge (dialect, cycles, number format and rounding, how the control tolerance is set, control-held values) belongs to NCXchange, as D-021 and D-031 already say for the dialect; where a machine cannot do what the NCX asks, NCXchange reports it and the CAM shows the report (`ncx check`). Where controllers differ, the CAM writes the form all of them accept, as D-061 does for cycles. Machine data still serves checks, simulation and time estimates (D-053, D-095) and the process limits of a job (spindle and feed limits, travel), never a controller-specific form of the output. A-061 (Fanuc control tolerance) is therefore NCXchange's question (T-039) | accepted | [CP-009] |
| D-149 | 2026-10-01 | Output rounding in NCXchange (under D-148): the CAM writes the NCX from the unrounded record at one fixed resolution for every machine, 0.000001 mm (0.0000001 inch) for lengths and 0.0000001° for angles (ours), and states each operation's rounding allowance, the reserve of 0.1·tol (D-056). NCXchange rounds once to each machine file's decimals, keeps arc centres and radii consistent, computes the end points the control will compute (Heidenhain CP IPA, Siemens AR=, Fanuc I and J) and keeps them within half an output step, and reports any rounding beyond the allowance (T-039). Four decimals in mm stay the recommendation for controls that accept them, above all for 3D smoothing; that is machine-file data. D-146's reserve becomes 0.1·tol without the floor from the output step, so the CAM's smallest tolerance follows from the grid alone, about 0.0023 mm; a machine whose rounding does not fit the allowance is reported by NCXchange (for example below about 0.0071 mm at 0.001 mm output, 0.018 mm at 0.0001 inch). D-063's round trip compares the read-back program with the record within the operation's allowance. Supersedes D-147; amends D-146, D-029 and D-063 | accepted | [CP-009] |
